GOOD QUESTIONS FIRST

Before you
take the next step.

Here are a few helpful starting points. Our team can share the latest official documents for the details that matter to you.

Where is Shahrukhz Residences located?

The project is presented as a waterfront residential development in Dubai Maritime City, Dubai, UAE.

What types of homes are available?

The developer lists studios, 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom residences, as well as penthouses. Specific layouts and availability should be confirmed.

Are the residences furnished?

Developer materials describe the residences as fully furnished. Please request the official furnishing and specification details for the exact unit.

What is the payment plan?

The currently published marketing plan is 30/70. Exact instalment dates, eligibility, fees and terms must be confirmed in the official payment schedule and sale agreement.

When is the expected handover?

Developer marketing material states Q4 2029. Dates can change, so please verify the latest handover date in the official documents before booking.

How can I get the brochure and latest prices?

Complete the enquiry form and select your preferred residence. Karwan Properties can follow up with the requested project information after you configure the contact details for this landing page.
