Privacy Notice

This notice explains how information submitted through the Karwan Properties website may be handled.

Karwan Properties respects your privacy. This notice describes the types of information you may provide when enquiring about Shahrukhz Residences by Danube or other property opportunities, how that information may be used, and the circumstances in which it may be shared.

This notice should be read alongside the actual data collection and handling practices used by Karwan Properties.

1. Information We May Collect

When you submit a property enquiry through our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your telephone or WhatsApp number.
  • Your email address, if requested by the form.
  • Your property interests and enquiry preferences.
  • Your purchase objectives and approximate budget, if provided.
  • Technical information associated with your enquiry, such as the page from which it was submitted and the submission time.

Please provide only information that is relevant to your property enquiry. Do not submit payment details, passwords, or other sensitive personal information through the enquiry form.

2. How We May Use Your Information

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to your property enquiry.
  • Provide the project information, availability details, or payment plan information you have requested.
  • Contact you regarding your enquiry using the contact details you provided.
  • Understand your stated property preferences and direct your enquiry to the appropriate team member.
  • Maintain enquiry records and help manage the operation of the website.

Any additional use of your information should have an appropriate legal basis and, where required, your consent.

3. WhatsApp and Other Third-Party Services

If you choose to continue your enquiry through WhatsApp, the information you include in your message will be processed through WhatsApp and may be subject to its own terms and privacy practices.

4. How Information Is Shared

Personal information may be shared with authorised personnel or service providers where reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, operate the website, or provide a service you have requested.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a lawful request from a competent authority.

Personal information should not be sold to third parties for their independent marketing purposes without an appropriate legal basis and any consent required by law.

5. Data Storage and Security

Enquiry information may be stored using the systems configured for this website. Appropriate technical and organisational measures should be used to protect that information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Access to enquiry information should be limited to people who need it for legitimate business purposes.

6. Data Retention

Personal information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to handle the enquiry, maintain relevant business records, meet applicable legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

The actual retention period depends on the purpose for which the information was collected and the legal requirements that apply.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights to request access to your personal information, correct inaccurate details, request deletion, object to certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

To make a privacy-related request, contact Karwan Properties using the verified contact details provided on its official website. Additional information may be required to confirm your identity before a request can be processed.

8. Cookies and Website Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies if these have been configured. Such technologies may support website functionality, security, performance measurement, or advertising measurement.

Any cookies or tracking technologies actually used on the website should be disclosed here, together with information about available controls and any consent required by applicable law.

9. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or services, including messaging platforms. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites. Please review their privacy notices before providing personal information through those services.

10. Changes to This Notice

This notice may be updated when website functionality, information handling practices, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated notice will be published on this page.

Privacy Enquiries

For questions about this notice or the handling of information submitted through the Karwan Properties website, please use the verified contact details published on the official website.
